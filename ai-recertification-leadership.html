Navigating a Complex Building Recertification with AI

Applying AI across technical research, contractor evaluation, financial analysis, and stakeholder communications.

01 — The challenge

A High-Stakes Project Outside My Expertise.

I serve as President of a 14-unit condominium association in Miami Beach, Florida. In 2025, a 50-year building recertification put structural and electrical assessments, contractor procurement, capital improvement planning, financing, and homeowner communications in front of me at once.

None of it is my professional field. The decisions were consequential, the information came from specialists, and homeowners needed to understand the plan and what it would cost.

  • Structural & electrical assessments
  • Contractor procurement
  • Capital improvement planning
  • Financing
  • Homeowner communications
02 — AI in action

A Thinking Partner, Not a Substitute for Experts.

Three ways I used conversational AI to understand, compare, and communicate. Open each to see the challenge, how I applied AI, and the related project artifact.

01

Understanding engineering and compliance requirementsTechnical findings, milestones, and questions for the experts.
The challenge
The engineering and compliance material was written for specialists. I needed to understand what it meant, what the recertification required, and what came next.
How AI was applied
I used conversational AI to explain technical findings in plain language, organize the recertification into milestones, and draft questions to bring to our engineers and other professionals.
What it produced
A working grasp of the process, an organized view of the milestones, and a prepared list of questions for each expert conversation.
Project artifact

Slide 4: the timeline presented to homeowners.

02

Evaluating proposals and identifying risksEngineering, construction, and roofing bids, side by side.
The challenge
Bids from engineering, construction, and roofing firms arrived in different formats, with different scopes and assumptions.
How AI was applied
I used AI to structure the proposals into a consistent comparison, surface differences in scope and assumptions, and turn gaps into questions to take back to the firms and our engineer.
What it produced
A side-by-side framework that supported board discussion. Contractor bids were reviewed under our engineer’s supervision.
Project artifact

Slide 10: contractor names and bid amounts are blurred.

03

Financial analysis and presentation developmentBudget, payment options, and a homeowner presentation.
The challenge
A project budget of about $296K, three possible financing and payment structures, and homeowners who needed to understand the trade-offs.
How AI was applied
I used AI to structure the financial information, examine the three payment options, and shape a clear, plain-language presentation for homeowners.
What it produced
A 20-slide presentation that set out the project budget and the three payment options side by side.
Project artifact

Slides 15 and 16. Dollar amounts on slide 16 are blurred.

Human expertise

AI complemented the work of licensed professionals. It did not replace professional engineering, legal, or financial expertise, and it did not validate their work.

03 — My takeaway

Leading Through Complexity.

The takeaway

AI didn’t replace the experts. It helped me ask better questions, make more informed decisions, and lead through complexity that extended well beyond my professional expertise.
