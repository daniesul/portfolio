Trade Shows & Conferences

  • My approach5 pillars ↓
  • Selected workCase studies ↓

I approach trade shows and conferences as investments in measurable business outcomes—from brand awareness and customer engagement to pipeline creation. I prioritize events and investment levels against those objectives, then bring teams and partners together to maximize our presence through coordinated campaigns and distinctive experiences. Each program connects the opportunity to reach our buyers with a clear plan to engage them, follow through, and evaluate results.

My approach

Five pillars
12345

Cross-functional collaboration runs across all five pillars — sales, marketing, partner, and creative teams working from one plan.

01Event IdentificationChoose events and investment levels aligned with our buyers and business goals.

I research where our target buyers and buying committees gather, then compare events against our marketing and business priorities. I recommend participation and investment levels based on audience fit, opportunities to reach prospects and customers, and expected contribution to brand awareness, relationships, and pipeline. For demand-focused programs, I work backward from revenue goals through conversion assumptions to understand what each investment needs to deliver. This informs annual planning and identifies the key events around which we align launches and campaigns.

02Integrated Event StrategyAlign teams, partners, and activities to maximize the opportunity.

I bring sales, marketing, and partner teams together to define success and build a coordinated plan for achieving it. I look across the full opportunity—sponsorship benefits, speaking sessions, pre-event outreach, customer meetings, partner collaboration, and ancillary events—to determine how the pieces can reinforce one another. My focus is on maximizing a moment when our buyers and ecosystem are in one place, directing budget and resources toward the activities most likely to advance our goals.

03Creative ActivationCreate distinctive experiences that attract the right people and make engagement valuable.

I develop experiences that give the right people a compelling reason to spend time with our brand during a crowded event week. Working with creative teams and partners, I connect booth engagement, content, and surrounding experiences to the audience’s interests and the program’s objectives. I consider both what will attract attention and what makes that interaction valuable—whether that means building awareness, starting a substantive conversation, or deepening a customer relationship.

04Post-Event Follow-UpCarry event engagement into relevant conversations and opportunities.

I build the follow-up strategy into planning, aligning sales and marketing on ownership, timing, and next steps. The approach should reflect who engaged, what they expressed interest in, and where they are in their relationship with us. My focus is on carrying the experience into relevant conversations that support pipeline creation, opportunity progression, or continued customer engagement, with clear accountability for what happens next.

05Measurement & ImprovementEvaluate outcomes, learn from execution, and strengthen future investments.

I evaluate each program against its original objectives, distinguishing immediate engagement from outcomes that develop over time. For pipeline-focused investments, that includes examining how engagement progresses into qualified leads, opportunities, and revenue against our planning assumptions. I also bring teams together to review the strategy, experience, and execution—what worked, what fell short, and why. Those findings shape recommendations on which events to repeat, where to adjust investment, and how to strengthen the next program.

Case study 03
Regional event program

Scaling Regional Cybersecurity Engagement

My roleStrategy & program lead

Built a scalable regional event marketing program leveraging practitioner-focused cybersecurity conferences to expand Sumo Logic’s presence in priority U.S. markets, reach new buyers, and create additional pipeline opportunities for regional sales teams.

Objective & approach

Addressed a key new-logo acquisition challenge by expanding our field presence beyond large industry conferences into smaller, locally focused cybersecurity events. Developed a 12-month strategy spanning 30+ events across Tier 1 and Tier 2 U.S. markets, testing two event providers to evaluate audience reach, engagement quality, lead conversion, and pipeline impact. Created a repeatable, cost-efficient activation model that enabled regional sales teams to engage cybersecurity practitioners without requiring dedicated on-site marketing resources.

Key components

  • Regional event strategy & optimizationBuilt a coordinated first-half and second-half event calendar targeting priority U.S. markets. Evaluated two event providers based on audience composition, attendee quality, engagement, and pipeline contribution to inform future investment decisions.
  • Turnkey booth & event activationDeveloped a standardized 10 × 10-foot booth kit with predefined messaging, presentation content, demo requirements, displays, branded materials, and swag. Designed for easy shipment and sales-led execution, enabling activation across 30+ events without dedicated marketing support on-site.
  • Speaking & practitioner engagementSecured speaking opportunities in select priority markets, complementing booth engagement with thought leadership and practical conversations tailored to local cybersecurity professionals.
  • Sales enablement & activationEquipped sales and SDR teams with standardized event playbooks, targeted outreach sequences, and customizable “Meet Me At” social content and templates. Simplified pre-event promotion and on-site execution to increase field participation while minimizing marketing resources.
  • Integrated follow-up & lead nurturingDeveloped segmented post-event engagement journeys based on lead qualification and buying intent. Combined targeted SDR outreach with personalized meeting incentives, including a complimentary lunch offer, and progressive nurture campaigns featuring product education, demos, and free trials to convert event engagement into sales conversations.
  • Scalable operating modelStandardized event planning, logistics, creative assets, sales resources, and post-event workflows to deliver a consistent brand experience across markets while reducing the operational burden on the central field marketing team.

Results

  • 30+Regional cybersecurity events activated over 12 months
  • 10,000+Cybersecurity contacts acquired
  • 600Marketing-qualified leads (MQLs) generated
  • $6MNew pipeline generated
  • $3MExisting pipeline influenced & accelerated

Pipeline forecasting model

Trade Show Pipeline Estimator

Developed a data-driven pipeline forecasting model to evaluate third-party event investments, establish measurable performance expectations, and align marketing and sales around anticipated pipeline contribution.

Objective & approach

Trade shows represent a significant investment, yet forecasting their impact can be challenging due to differences in audience size, sponsorship levels, engagement opportunities, and historical performance. To bring greater consistency and financial accountability to event planning, I developed a standardized pipeline estimator that translated event participation into projected lead generation, sales opportunities, and pipeline value. The model combined historical performance data, conversion assumptions, and event-specific inputs to support more informed investment decisions and establish measurable targets before committing budget.

Key components

  • Data-driven forecasting frameworkDeveloped a standardized model incorporating estimated attendance, audience engagement, MQL conversion, sales meeting conversion, opportunity creation rates, and average opportunity value to forecast potential pipeline contribution.
  • Scenario planning & investment evaluationEnabled marketing teams to evaluate different event participation scenarios, including sponsorship levels, booth presence, speaking opportunities, and ancillary activations, to understand potential impact and set realistic performance expectations.
  • Pipeline targets & sales alignmentTranslated event forecasts into measurable engagement, lead generation, meeting, and opportunity targets, creating shared accountability between marketing, sales, and SDR teams.
  • Budget planning & portfolio prioritizationIntroduced a consistent approach to comparing third-party event investments, helping prioritize opportunities based on projected pipeline contribution and alignment with regional business objectives.
  • Performance measurement & optimizationEstablished a framework for evaluating actual results against initial forecasts, identifying performance gaps, and refining assumptions to improve future event planning and investment decisions.

Results & business impact

  • Standardized pipeline forecasting across third-party event investment planning
  • Greater visibility into expected ROI before sponsorship commitments
  • Improved alignment between marketing investment and sales pipeline expectations
  • More consistent performance measurement across events and regions
