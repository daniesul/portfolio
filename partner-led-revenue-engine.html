With no dedicated resources or established operating model, I developed the blueprint, aligned cross-functional teams, and brought the program from concept to launch in just 30 days.

01

I Saw an Opportunity. The Partner Motion Didn’t Exist.

Sumo Logic had an established direct product trial experience, but no defined motion for partners to introduce prospects, track engagement, or translate trial activity into qualified Sales conversations.

I recognized an opportunity to extend an existing product experience into a new partner-enabled demand generation channel.

However, there was no dedicated owner, operating model, or allocated team to build it.

02

Building the Motion Without a Dedicated Team

The challenge wasn’t simply launching a new marketing campaign.

It required aligning Product, Engineering, Digital Marketing, Marketing Operations, Sales, and Partner teams—none of whom reported directly to me.

I personally developed the end-to-end program blueprint, mapped the required capabilities, built the business case, secured cross-functional commitment, defined ownership, and coordinated execution.

03

Blueprinting an End-to-End Partner Trial Engine

I designed the operating model connecting partner referral, product trial activation, behavioral engagement intelligence, and coordinated Sales follow-up.

This was more than a trial registration campaign. It connected multiple systems, teams, and processes into a repeatable partner-led demand generation motion.

  1. Stage 01
    Partner Referral
  2. Stage 02
    Trial Activation
  3. Stage 03
    Partner Reporting
  4. Stage 04
    AI-Assisted Usage Intelligence
  5. Stage 05
    Sales Opportunity Conversion
  1. Stage 01 · Partner Referral

    Unique, Trackable Partner URLs

    Worked with the Digital Marketing team to develop partner-specific trial links and tagging.

    These unique URLs allowed us to attribute trial activations to the referring partner and connect that information to underlying reporting.

    This established the foundation for partner-level acquisition visibility and follow-up.

  2. Stage 02 · Product Trial Activation

    14-Day Agentic AI Product Trial

    Coordinated with Product and Engineering to adapt the existing trial experience for partner-led acquisition.

    This included creating the necessary user experience, updating the interface, and supporting a guided trial experience.

    The 14-day trial gave prospects an opportunity to explore Sumo Logic’s Agentic AI capabilities.

  3. Stage 03 · Partner Activation Visibility

    Partner-Specific Trial Reporting

    Developed a process that informed partner sellers which prospects had activated trials using their unique referral links.

    This gave partners visibility into trial activation rather than leaving them dependent on generic lead reporting.

  4. Stage 04 · AI-Assisted Usage Intelligence

    Day-Six Product Engagement Reports

    Using Claude, I helped design and build a reporting process that transformed product usage data into actionable partner insights.

    Six days after trial activation, partners received an updated engagement report. The reporting included:

    • Which referred prospects were actively using their trials
    • High-level product engagement patterns
    • Data sources being explored
    • Indicators of potential customer interests and use cases

    These insights helped partner sellers tailor their conversations to the prospect’s demonstrated product interests.

  5. Stage 05 · Sales Qualification & Opportunity Creation

    From Product Interest to Sales-Accepted Opportunity

    Enabled partner sellers to use trial activation and product engagement intelligence to prioritize follow-up, tailor outreach, and coordinate with Sales.

    The objective was to move engaged trial participants into qualified conversations, product demonstrations, and ultimately sales-accepted opportunities.

04

Turning Product Usage Into Actionable Partner Intelligence

I wanted partners to have more than a list of trial registrations.

I wanted to equip them with insights into what their prospects were actually exploring, so they could have more relevant follow-up conversations.

Using Claude, I helped develop an AI-assisted reporting and automation process that transformed product usage data into structured engagement summaries.

These reports highlighted trial activity and data-source interests, helping partner sellers understand potential use cases and identify opportunities for deeper Sales engagement.

05

From Concept to Launch in 30 Days

With no dedicated implementation team, I worked across functions to establish the business case, build the required capabilities, enable partners, and launch the initial program in just 30 days.

We used a pilot-first approach, incorporating feedback from participating partners, Product, and Sales before expanding the motion.

06

From an Untapped Opportunity to a Scalable Partner Demand Engine

What began as an identified gap in our partner go-to-market strategy became a scalable demand generation engine connecting product trials, partner attribution, AI-assisted engagement intelligence, and coordinated Sales activation.

Launched in just 30 days, the program subsequently scaled to more than 100 partners, generated over 5,000 product trials, and achieved a reported 20% sales-accepted opportunity conversion rate, contributing to significant closed new-logo revenue.
