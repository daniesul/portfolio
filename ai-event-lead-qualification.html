02 — How it worked

Built for Scale. Designed for Human Oversight.

The workflow

Not just a prompt. A repeatable process.

  1. 01

    Event Lead Data

    Scans, responses, demos, notes.

    Details

    Booth scans, qualification responses, demo participation, conversation notes, and engagement signals.

  2. 02
    AI · The core

    Custom Claude Skill

    Reads intent, applies criteria, recommends a status.

    Details

    AI analyzes buyer intent, evaluates qualification criteria, translates multilingual notes, standardizes data, and recommends lead classifications. One skill, shared across the field marketing team.

  3. 03
    Human checkpoint 1

    Field Marketing Validation

    A field marketer reviews the recommended statuses.

    Details

    A field marketer reviews and validates AI-recommended lead statuses, engagement context, and follow-up actions.

  4. 04
    Automation

    Automated Data Preparation

    Formats the Marketo import template.

    Details

    An automated workflow converts the qualified list into the required Marketo import template and posts it directly to the central data repository.

  5. 05
    Human checkpoint 2

    Marketing Operations Validation

    Final quality check before upload.

    Details

    Marketing Operations completes a final quality check before initiating the Marketo upload.

  6. 06

    Marketo Activation

    Leads and context ready for SDR follow-up.

    Details

    Qualified leads and standardized engagement context become available for SDR follow-up.

04 — Leadership philosophy

My Approach to AI

  1. 01

    Automate

    Eliminate repetitive lead processing, data formatting, and manual administrative work.

  2. 02

    Augment

    Improve decision-making through consistent qualification, buyer-signal analysis, and multilingual intelligence.

  3. 03

    Elevate

    Give marketing teams more time for customers, sales partnership, experimentation, and strategy.

The principle

AI creates the greatest value when it improves how teams work—not simply how quickly they complete tasks.
