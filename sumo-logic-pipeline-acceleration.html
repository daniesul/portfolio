Turning Stalled Pipeline Into Revenue Opportunity

I led an intent-informed pipeline acceleration initiative that combined Salesforce opportunity intelligence, third-party buying signals, and persona-specific marketing to reignite engagement and move stalled opportunities toward proof-of-value (POV).

  • 400+Stalled Opportunities Identified
  • 90+DaysIn the Same Sales Stage
  • 40Opportunities Advanced to POV

The initiative at a glance

How the initiative worked

  1. CRM Intelligence + Third-Party Intent

    Salesforce opportunity data and account-level buying signals.

  2. Prioritized Opportunities

    Buying stage, persona, use case, and intent.

  3. Persona-Led Activation

    Email, LinkedIn, executive and peer engagement, and Sales-aligned field activation.

  4. POV Progression

    Stalled opportunities re-engaged and moved toward proof-of-value.

01

The Challenge: Pipeline Without Progression

More than 400 opportunities had remained in the same sales stage for 90+ days, particularly between demo and proof-of-value (POV).

With opportunities reaching POV historically achieving an 80%+ close rate, advancing qualified stalled deals into this stage represented a significant revenue opportunity.

The challenge wasn’t generating more leads. It was identifying where buying interest remained active and deploying targeted engagement to help opportunities move forward.

02

Combining Pipeline Intelligence With Buying Intent

I partnered with regional Sales leaders to identify and prioritize stalled opportunities, combining Salesforce opportunity data with third-party digital intent signals to understand which accounts continued to demonstrate active buying interest.

This gave us a more informed way to prioritize re-engagement and tailor marketing interventions around each opportunity’s buying context.

Source 01

Salesforce Pipeline Intelligence

  • 90+ days in the current sales stage
  • Opportunity value and stage
  • Sales engagement history
  • Account and opportunity context
Source 02

Third-Party Intent Intelligence

  • Continued solution research
  • Relevant use-case interests
  • Competitor research activity
  • Broader account-level buying signals
↓

Prioritized Opportunity Re-engagement

Buying Stage + Persona + Use Case + Intent

This initiative went beyond identifying aging opportunities in Salesforce. We combined pipeline intelligence with digital buying behavior to better understand where interest remained active and how to engage buyers with more relevant messaging and experiences.

Third-party intent represents account-level buying indicators, not the actions of specific individuals.

03

Persona-Led, Multichannel Re-engagement

We translated pipeline and intent insights into coordinated marketing plays tailored to each opportunity’s buying stage, identified use case, and key stakeholders.

  • Illustrative
    Play 01

    Persona-Specific Email Nurture

    Developed dedicated email nurture journeys with tailored messaging and relevant content to address buying barriers, reinforce solution value, and encourage renewed engagement.

  • Illustrative
    Play 02

    Targeted LinkedIn Advertising

    Activated account- and persona-targeted LinkedIn campaigns, aligning messaging and creative to buying roles, identified use cases, solution interests, and relevant competitive signals.

  • Illustrative
    Play 03

    Executive & Peer Engagement

    Leveraged executive dinners, customer advocacy, and peer-to-peer conversations to build confidence, address concerns, and reconnect decision makers with Sales.

  • Illustrative
    Play 04

    Sales-Aligned Field Activation

    Coordinated account-specific outreach and regional field marketing programs with Sales owners to create relevant engagement opportunities and support movement toward POV.

↓
The common objective Reignite Buyer Engagement → Advance to POV

These were not four disconnected marketing tactics. They formed a coordinated, account-level re-engagement strategy informed by what Sales knew about each opportunity and what digital buying behavior suggested about ongoing interest.

04

Turning Re-engagement Into Pipeline Progression

The initiative helped move approximately 40 previously stalled opportunities into proof-of-value, representing approximately $4M in associated pipeline.

By combining pipeline intelligence, third-party intent, personalized digital engagement, and Sales-aligned activation, we established a more targeted approach to opportunity acceleration.

  • 40Stalled Opportunities Advanced to POV
  • ~$4MAssociated Opportunity Pipeline
  • ~10%Of Identified Stalled Opportunities Advanced
  • Advanced to POV means progression to proof-of-value, not closed-won.
  • Associated pipeline is opportunity value, not realized revenue.
  • ~10% is approximate, measured against the 400+ opportunities identified.
