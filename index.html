My approach

I believe effective marketing starts with understanding the business challenge—not simply executing the next campaign.

My approach combines strategic planning, creative thinking, data-driven decision-making, and cross-functional collaboration to develop marketing programs and operating models that create meaningful business impact.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Start with the business challenge, customer needs, commercial priorities, and available resources.

  2. 02

    Challenge

    Question existing assumptions, identify opportunities, and explore better ways of working.

  3. 03

    Build

    Translate strategy into coordinated programs, scalable frameworks, and executable plans.

  4. 04

    Evolve

    Measure performance, learn from the results, and continuously refine the approach.

How I lead

A transformational servant leader.

Inspiring change. Empowering people to make it happen.

I believe meaningful transformation starts with people. My leadership style combines a vision for challenging the status quo and inspiring innovation with a commitment to understanding each individual’s strengths, interests, and aspirations. I focus on bridging the gap between where people are today and where they have the potential to go—equipping them with the skills, confidence, and opportunities to grow, think differently, and drive meaningful change.

  • 01 / Transformational

    Inspire what’s next.
