01

Recognizing a Market Inflection Point

In May 2024, two established SIEM competitors announced significant strategic changes:

  • IBM announced the sale of its QRadar SaaS assets to Palo Alto Networks.
  • LogRhythm announced its merger with Exabeam.

These developments created an opportunity to engage organizations reassessing their existing security investments, product roadmaps, and potential migration strategies.

I recognized the market disruption as an opportunity to move beyond broad competitive marketing and build a more focused acquisition strategy around accounts with a potential reason to evaluate alternatives.

02

From Market Disruption to Precision Targeting

Rather than approach every competitive account with the same campaign, I partnered with Sales to build a prioritized acquisition strategy based on account fit, competitive technology installations, buying signals, and potential migration timing.

We evaluated more than 1,000 potential competitive accounts and narrowed our focus to 120 priority targets.

1,000+ competitive accounts evaluated, through four intelligence inputs

  1. 01Technographic Intelligence

    Existing SIEM platforms and competitive technology installations.

  2. 02Third-Party Buying Intent

    Active research activity, solution interests, and competitive buying signals.

  3. 03Ideal Customer Profile & Fit

    Industry, account characteristics, and revenue potential.

  4. 04Migration & Renewal Timing

    Potential contract renewal windows, platform transitions, and migration considerations.

↓

120 Priority Accounts

Selected for targeted competitive displacement activation.

03

Different Investment Levels. One Coordinated Strategy.

I developed a tiered account-based marketing approach that aligned investment, personalization, and engagement tactics to each account’s strategic value and buying signals.

Our 120 priority accounts were organized into three activation tiers.

↑ Deepest personalization and investment per account

Tier 1

Strategic 1:1 ABM

10Accounts

Highest-priority competitive displacement targets.

Activation approach
  • Individual account engagement plans
  • Executive sponsorship and engagement
  • Tailored account messaging
  • Relevant partner introductions
  • Exclusive executive experiences
Objective

Build deeper relationships and create opportunities for highly personalized competitive conversations.

Tier 2

Targeted 1:Few ABM

25Accounts

Accounts grouped around shared industry needs, competitive challenges, and solution use cases.

Activation approach
  • Persona-specific email nurture
  • Targeted LinkedIn campaigns
  • Industry and use-case messaging
  • Tailored solution content
  • Coordinated Sales outreach
Objective

Create relevant engagement around common buying needs while maintaining meaningful personalization.

Tier 3

Scaled 1:Many ABM

85Accounts

A broader set of qualified competitive accounts activated through repeatable digital programs.

Activation approach
  • Targeted digital advertising
  • Email nurture
  • Educational webinars
  • Relevant competitive and solution content
  • Coordinated SDR engagement
Objective

Build awareness, identify emerging interest, and create scalable paths to Sales engagement.

↓ Broadest reach through repeatable digital programs

10 + 25 + 85 = 120 priority accounts

Not every account received the same activation: engagement varied by tier, account priority, and buying signals.

04

Connecting Competitive Intelligence to Buyer Engagement

Across all three tiers, we tailored engagement to the buying group, competitive context, and identified solution interests.

Rather than relying on a single channel, we coordinated Sales, Marketing, partners, and executive engagement to create relevant conversations at the account level.

  • Persona-Based Messaging

    Tailored value propositions for security executives, technical evaluators, and operational practitioners.

  • Digital Demand Activation

    Targeted LinkedIn advertising, email nurture, and relevant solution content.

  • Executive & Partner Engagement

    Executive experiences, strategic introductions, and trusted partner relationships.

  • Sales-Aligned Outreach

    Account-specific planning, SDR coordination, and personalized follow-up informed by buying signals.

↓
Engage → Qualify → Create New-Logo Opportunity One integrated account-level motion
05

Precision Targeting. Meaningful New-Logo Progress.

By translating competitive market disruption into a focused ABM strategy, we prioritized 120 high-potential accounts and executed differentiated engagement across three investment tiers.

The initiative generated 20 new-logo sales opportunities and secured five new-logo customer wins at the time of reporting.

Beyond these outcomes, the program established a repeatable approach to identifying and activating competitive displacement opportunities through market intelligence, buying signals, and coordinated Sales and Marketing execution.
