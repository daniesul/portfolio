AI Innovation / Data & Reporting

Breaking Down Technical Barriers with AI

Using Claude to bridge data gaps, build custom reporting, and deliver more personalized intelligence to Marketing and Sales, without needing to be a developer.

I've always understood the business problem, the data I wanted, and the experience I wanted to create. What I often lacked was the Python, Apps Script and data-transformation skill to build it. Claude closed that gap, so I can now develop, troubleshoot and refine solutions that once needed a technical specialist.

“I always knew what data I wanted and how it could better support Marketing and Sales. AI gave me the technical capabilities to turn those ideas into working solutions.”
01 — The shift

From Technical Dependency to Greater Self-Sufficiency

Before AI

Constrained by technical dependencies

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4

    Potentially compromise on the desired solution.

With Claude

Empowered to build

  1. 1

  3. 3

  4. 4

    Test, troubleshoot, and refine the solution myself.

04 — Behind the build

Working Confidently with Tools That Once Created Barriers

  • Python
  • Google Apps Script
  • Google Sheets
  • Data transformations
  • Custom reporting logic

I'm a marketing leader, not a software engineer. Claude helps me work confidently with technologies that previously created barriers.

  1. Me

    Define

    I set the business requirement, the data needed and the output I want.

  2. Claude

    Draft

    Claude proposes the logic and writes and explains the code or formulas.

  3. Me

    Test

    I run it, check results and troubleshoot issues with Claude.

  4. Me

    Refine

    I iterate and validate until the solution works for the team.

How I work with AI. I own the business strategy, requirements, interpretation and decisions. Claude assists with technical implementation. AI-generated code and outputs require validation, so I review, test and refine everything before it is relied on.

05 — The impact

Turning Ideas into Execution

AI has fundamentally expanded my technical self-sufficiency. I'm no longer limited to the reporting structures already available or dependent on specialized development resources for every new idea.

By combining my knowledge of marketing and sales operations with Claude-assisted technical development, I can build more customized, connected, and relevant data solutions directly.

  • 01

    Greater Autonomy

    More ability to develop and refine solutions without relying entirely on technical specialists.

  • 02

    Better Data Accessibility

    Greater flexibility to connect information and make it useful across teams.

  • 03

    More Relevant Intelligence

    Reporting and shared resources designed around actual Marketing and Sales needs.

Final takeaway

“AI didn't just help me analyze data faster. It gave me the ability to build the reporting, tools, and personalized insights I had always wanted to provide, but previously lacked the technical resources to create.”
