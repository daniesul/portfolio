Cisco | Audience Strategy & Campaign Transformation

From Fragmented Personas to One Unified Audience Strategy

I led the evolution of Cisco’s audience strategy, bringing fragmented persona definitions into a unified framework that enabled more consistent, targeted, and connected campaign activation across business units, solutions, and marketing channels.

01

The challenge

Across Cisco’s business units, teams had developed their own buyer personas, creating fragmented audience definitions and inconsistent approaches to campaign activation.

As Cisco evolved toward an integrated, solution-led campaign strategy, we needed a common understanding of who we were targeting, what mattered to them, and how best to engage them.

02

The strategy: three core persona categories

I led the development of a standardized audience framework built around three primary persona categories that guided how marketing teams planned campaigns, built audiences, and tailored engagement.

  • Persona 01

    Decision Makers

    Strategic priorities, business outcomes, risk, and investment value.

  • Persona 02

    Recommenders

    Solution evaluation, technical influence, and purchase recommendations.

  • Persona 03

    Practitioners

    Implementation, operational efficiency, and day-to-day technology needs.

03

Turning insights into action

My team translated the audience framework into three comprehensive Persona Activation Playbooks, providing practical guidance for how marketing teams could engage each audience across Cisco’s business units and solution portfolio.

Each playbook connected buyer insights to the content, channels, and touchpoints most relevant throughout the buying journey.

  • Buyer Insights

    Motivations, challenges, priorities, and decision drivers.

  • Journey Strategy

    Audience needs across awareness, consideration, purchase, and retention.

  • Content & Channels

    Relevant messaging, content formats, engagement experiences, and touchpoints.

  • Campaign Activation

    Practical recommendations for activating personas across solutions and business units.

Actionable marketing resources, not just research documents.

04

Operationalizing the strategy

Beyond developing the playbooks, we embedded standardized persona classifications directly into Cisco’s marketing contact database and underlying data foundation.

This helped teams consistently define, build, and activate target audiences across marketing platforms and campaigns, from selecting appropriate job titles and contacts to delivering persona-relevant campaigns.
