AI Innovation / Marketing Operations — Sumo Logic

Building a Global Marketing Planning Engine with AI

From marketing strategy to a functional planning system, using AI as a technical collaborator.

I’m a marketing leader, not an engineer. To give global Field & Partner Marketing one planning system across AMER, EMEA, and APJ, I used Claude to help me design, build, and refine it in Google Sheets. I owned the strategy, the requirements, and every decision. AI helped me turn them into working structure and formulas.

  • CompanySumo Logic
  • My roleStrategy, requirements, validation, and hands-on build with AI
  • RegionsAMER · EMEA · APJ
01 — The challenge

One system for three regions.

Managing marketing investments across AMER, EMEA, and APJ required one place for planning, data capture, budget allocation, and performance visibility.

Building it meant turning complex business requirements into scalable spreadsheet architecture, calculations, and standardized workflows.

  • Planning
  • Data capture
  • Budget allocation
  • Performance visibility
02 — How I leveraged AI

A technical collaborator, hands-on.

  • 01

    Spreadsheet architecture

    I used AI to design and rebuild Google Sheets templates, organize planning data, and set up scalable structures across regions and teams.

  • 02

    Formula development

    I used AI to write, troubleshoot, and refine formulas, automate calculations, and connect data across planning worksheets.

  • 03

    Data capture & planning logic

    I worked with AI to turn marketing requirements into standardized inputs, investment categories, prioritization logic, and reporting structures.

  • 04

    Iterative development

    I used conversational AI to test ideas, resolve technical issues, rebuild templates, and keep refining the system.
