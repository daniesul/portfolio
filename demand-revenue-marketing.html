My approach / Account-based marketing

Account-Based Marketing

Smarter investment. Meaningful engagement. Measurable growth.

I believe Account-Based Marketing is fundamentally about making smarter decisions about where to invest, how to create value, and how to align teams around the accounts and opportunities that matter most.

My approach combines market intelligence, revenue potential, buyer behavior, and customer insights to prioritize investment and deliver differentiated engagement strategies.

Effective ABM goes beyond identifying target accounts or personalizing campaigns. It’s about building preference before buying intent emerges, enabling entire buying groups, and orchestrating relevant experiences across marketing, sales, partners, and customer success.

I see ABM as a dynamic growth strategy that continuously adapts to account readiness, revenue opportunity, and customer needs—connecting investment to measurable outcomes across acquisition, acceleration, and expansion.

The Six Principles Behind My Approach

A continuous operating framework for translating account intelligence into coordinated engagement and measurable revenue outcomes.

  1. 01→ 02

    Understand the Opportunity

    Assess the market, ideal customer profile, whitespace, competitive landscape, customer needs, and account revenue potential.

  2. 02→ 03

    Prioritize Intelligently

    Combine account fit, intent, behavioral signals, and revenue potential to determine where to focus investment.

  3. 03→ 04

    Build Preference & Create Demand

    Establish relevance, familiarity, and trust before buying intent emerges, while creating demand across priority accounts.

  4. 04→ 05

    Enable the Buying Group

    Identify and understand stakeholders, build consensus, address barriers, and support confident purchasing decisions.

  5. 05→ 06

    Activate & Orchestrate

    Align marketing, sales, field, partners, and customer success around coordinated, personalized engagement strategies.

  6. 06↺ 01

    Measure, Learn & Optimize

    Evaluate account progression, buying-group engagement, pipeline, revenue, and investment performance to continuously improve.

A continuous cycle — what I learn in 06 informs how I understand the opportunity in 01.

An Adaptive Approach to Investment

Not every account requires the same level of engagement or investment. I use differentiated 1:1, 1:few, and 1:many motions that align resources with account revenue potential, buying readiness, and growth opportunity.

  • 1:1

    Strategic Engagement

    High-touch, highly personalized programs designed around individual accounts and their buying groups.

  • 1:Few

    Segment-Based Engagement

    Tailored programs for clusters of accounts sharing similar needs, buying triggers, or industry priorities.

  • 1:Many

    Scaled Engagement

    Data-informed, repeatable engagement that builds relevance and demand across broader priority-account segments.

Deeper personalizationBroader scale

These aren’t fixed classifications. Accounts move between motions as signals, buying readiness, and revenue opportunity change, and investment moves with them.

The operating principle

Intelligence Informs. Performance Refines.

↺Continuous feedback loop

ABM shouldn’t operate from a static annual account list. Account intelligence, engagement signals, buying-stage progression, and performance insights should continually inform which accounts receive investment, how engagement evolves, and where resources can deliver the greatest impact.

This continuous feedback loop is what makes ABM an adaptive, measurable growth strategy rather than a collection of disconnected campaigns.
